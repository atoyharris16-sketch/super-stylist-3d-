# 💖 Super Stylist 3D

A cute 3D dress-up game for young stylists (ages 4–8). Style a chibi doll —
pick her skin, eyes, brows, hair, outfits, shoes, socks, jewels and extras —
then spin her around in 360° and save a picture of your creation!

## How to play
- **Tap** big emoji cards to dress the doll — no reading required, no timers, no losing.
- **Drag** the doll to spin her around, **pinch / scroll** to zoom.
- **🔄** toggles auto-spin, **🎲** gives a surprise look, **↺** starts over,
  **📸** saves a PNG picture, **🔊** toggles sound.
- Name your doll on the little flower plaque. 🌸

## Run it
Just open `index.html` — it works from `file://` too (needs internet for the Three.js CDN).

## Put it on GitHub Pages
1. Create a new repo and upload `index.html` to the repo root.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**,
   pick your branch (e.g. `main`) and folder **/(root)**, then **Save**.
4. Your game will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/` in a minute or two. 🎉
