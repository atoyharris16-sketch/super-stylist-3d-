# 💖 Super Stylist

A polished dress-up game for young stylists (ages 4–8). Style a fashion-illustration
doll — pick her skin, eyes, brows, hair (with real strand shading), outfits, shoes,
socks, jewels and extras — then save a picture of your creation!

## How to play
- **Tap** big emoji cards to dress the doll — no reading required, no timers, no losing.
- **🎲** gives a surprise look, **↺** starts over, **📸** saves a PNG picture,
  **🔊** toggles sound.
- Name your doll on the little plaque. 💖
- Works great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

## Run it
Just open `index.html` — everything is in that one file, no internet needed.

## Put it on GitHub Pages
1. Upload `index.html` to the repo root.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**,
   pick your branch (e.g. `main`) and folder **/(root)**, then **Save**.
4. Your game will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/` in a minute or two. 🎉
